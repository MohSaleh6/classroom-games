🎁 Mystery Box: Word Challenge!

🎮 Student game 📺 Big-screen robot wall

How it works

Everything happens on the website. Students do not need Microsoft Word. Each mystery box opens a pretend Word window with a short page about “My Dream Gaming Room”. Students use its ribbon buttons to insert pictures, shapes and 3D models. There is no chart and no typing of the report.

There are 6 boxes and 10 minutes. Each box shows goals. They turn green when the work is right. The student presses Check ⭐ to get a star and a robot part. After 6 boxes the robot is complete and they get a Word Champion badge.

  • The game checks the work. A box only gives a star when every goal is green, so the class list shows real progress.
  • 10-minute game. The clock starts when a student presses Start! Open the boxes. A refresh does not reset it. At 0 the game ends: the student sees their robot with the parts they collected and a Robot Builder badge. All 6 boxes in time = Word Champion badge.
  • No points and no penalties. Boxes open in order. Students can open finished boxes again.
  • Each box has a 💡 Where is it in real Word? button. It shows the real Word menu path (for example Insert › Pictures), so students learn where the buttons are in Word.
  • Surprises: after Box 2 they pick their robot’s colour; after Box 4 they pick a celebration dance.
  • Support mode (🤝 button): bigger text, hints open, an extra hint in every box, and a little more room for the picture size and the 3D angles.
  • Progress is saved on the device. A refresh carries on where the student was.

👩‍🏫 Teacher panel in the game

  • On the student page, click 👩‍🏫 Teacher at the bottom and type the teacher PIN.
  • Class: every student and the boxes they finished, with a Support tag. A “⏰ Time up” tag shows who ran out of time. When a student shows you their Word Champion badge, press Confirm badge. Their badge screen then shows “Checked by Ms Rahaf” and the wall shows ✅.
  • Reset next to a name removes that student; their game starts again with a new 10 minutes. Use it if a student typed the wrong name or changed computers.
  • Reset tab: reset the whole class (before a new lesson) or reset only the device you are using.
  • The tabs also show this 45-minute plan and the answers for each box.
