🎁 Mystery Box: Word Challenge!

🎮 Student game 📺 Big-screen robot wall

How it works

Students make one short Word report about “My Dream Gaming Room” in real Microsoft Word. The website guides them through 6 mystery boxes, one task at a time, in 10 minutes. Each box they finish gives a star and a robot part. After 6 boxes their robot is complete and they get a Word Champion badge.

  • 10-minute game. The clock starts when a student presses Start! Open the boxes. A refresh does not reset it. At 0 the game ends: the student sees their robot with the parts they collected and a Robot Builder badge. All 6 boxes in time = Word Champion badge.
  • No points and no penalties. Boxes open in order. Students can open finished boxes again.
  • “I’ve finished” is the student’s own report. The website cannot see their Word file. Use the checklist below to check the real work.
  • Surprises: after Box 2 they pick their robot’s colour; after Box 4 they pick a celebration dance.
  • Support mode (🤝 button): bigger text, hints open, extra tips, and a ready-formatted starter text. Boxes 1–2 become quick “just check it” boxes, so these students spend their time on pictures, the table, the chart and checking.
  • Box 4 Plan B: if Word has no 3D Models, students insert an icon instead. The class list shows a “Plan B icon” tag.
  • Progress is saved on the device. A refresh carries on where the student was.

👩‍🏫 Teacher panel in the game

  • On the student page, click 👩‍🏫 Teacher at the bottom and type the teacher PIN.
  • Class: every student, the boxes they say they finished, Support / Plan B tags. A “⏰ Time up” tag shows who ran out of time. When all six are ticked and you have checked the Word file, press Confirm badge. The student’s badge screen then shows “Checked by Ms Rahaf” and the wall shows ✅.
  • Reset next to a name removes that student; their game starts again with a new 10 minutes. Use it if a student typed the wrong name or changed computers.
  • Reset tab: reset the whole class (before a new lesson) or reset only the device you are using.
  • The tabs also show this 45-minute plan, the spelling corrections and the checklist.

🖨️ Class checklist

Tick each box after you check it in the student’s Word file. Badge = all six correct and badge confirmed.
