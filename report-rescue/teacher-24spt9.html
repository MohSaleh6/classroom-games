Grade 7 · Unit 1: Creating a document

Report Rescue: Save the School Magazine!

Students fix a messy magazine report inside a pretend Word window in the browser. No Microsoft Word is needed. The buttons and tab names match real Word, so the skills transfer. The game checks every answer itself and sends each student’s score to one class leaderboard automatically.

45-minute lesson

⏱ Lesson plan

MinutesWhat happens
0–5Show the big-screen board and the prizes. Explain: 15 minutes, 5 missions, one play each. Most points wins; same points, faster time wins.
5–22Students play (15-minute timer inside the game, plus 2 minutes to start).
22–30Big-screen board: celebrate the top 3. Ms Rahaf gives the prizes.
30–45Discuss: which mission was hardest? Optional: open real Word and repeat one mission (e.g. Wrap Text: Square, or Insert → Table).

  • Each task: 10 points if right on the first Check with no hint, 5 points after a wrong Check or a hint.
  • Spelling: 2 points per word found alone; 1 point if the student used the Spelling Check button or chose a wrong spelling first.
  • Goals turn green as students work, so they always know what is left.
  • Ranks: 180+ 🏆 Chief Editor · 140+ 🌟 Senior Editor · 100+ ✏️ Junior Editor · below 100 📰 Trainee Editor.
  • Leaderboard: most points first; equal points → faster time first. Top 3: 🥇 +5 marks, sweet treat, 10 min free time · 🥈 +3 marks, 10 min · 🥉 10 min.
  • One play per name. If the page is closed by accident, opening it again on the same device continues the game.
