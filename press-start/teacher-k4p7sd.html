The idea

Students are the new designer at PRESS START, the school gaming magazine. You (Ms Rahaf) are the Editor-in-Chief. You send them 11 jobs across 5 magazine pages, one page per lesson topic. Each job shows a brief: a preview of how the page must look. Students use Draft, a pretend Word editor in the browser (no real Word needed), to make the page match. When everything matches, the page is stamped APPROVED.

There is no timer. When the student prints the issue in job 11, the game ends, the score is saved to the class leaderboard automatically, and the student sees their printed magazine. Most students finish in about 8–15 minutes.

Scoring and prizes

  • Each approved job: 100 points + up to 50 speed bonus (full bonus within 15 seconds, none after 75 seconds).
  • Combo: jobs in a row without a hint give +10, +20 … up to +50 extra.
  • Show me how (hint): −30 on that job, and the combo resets. It shows what to click and glows the right button.
  • Wrong spelling choice: −10 each.
  • Skip job (after a hint or 45 seconds): 0 points, Ms Rahaf finishes the job so the student can continue.
  • Highest possible score is about 2,050. Ranks: 1700+ Art Director · 1300+ Senior Designer · 900+ Designer · 500+ Junior Designer.
  • Leaderboard: most points first; equal points → faster time first.
  • Prizes: 1st +5 bonus marks, a sweet treat and 10 minutes free time · 2nd +3 bonus marks and 10 minutes free time · 3rd 10 minutes free time.

Suggested 45-minute lesson

MinutesWhat happens
0–5Show the big-screen leaderboard and the prizes. Explain: 11 jobs, one play per name, faster jobs earn a small speed bonus.
5–20Students play. There is no timer: tell students to use Skip if they are stuck.
20–28Leaderboard on the projector. Celebrate the top 3.
28–45Go through the answer key together: which job was hardest? Students explain where each tool is.

Teacher tools

  • Open Class leaderboard on the student page, then Teacher tools, and type the teacher PIN (the same PIN as the other games).
  • Remove a name to let that student play again (for example a typo in the name).
  • Clear the whole board before a new class.
  • Progress is saved on the computer, so a refresh continues the game.
